Skyscribe privacy policy

Skyscribe: RPG Weather, by Kurt Wirth, doing business as Wirthwhile Apps. Effective October 23, 2026.

Skyscribe is a weather generator for tabletop role-playing games, on Android and on the web. You can use all of it without an account. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information.

Without signing in

Skyscribe has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. If you don't sign in, we don't collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information. Your campaigns, the weather they have generated, place names, calendars and settings are saved only on your device: on your phone in the app, or in your browser on the web.

On Android, if you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include them in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Google; we never see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's data for the web app, deletes this data from that device.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets your campaigns follow you between your phone and your computer. You sign in with your Google account; we never see your Google password. When you sign in, we receive and keep:

  • your name, email address and an account identifier from Google, so we know which campaigns are yours;
  • a copy of your campaigns and settings, so they can be synced between your devices.

This information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States, and is used only to run your account and sync your campaigns. Each account's campaigns can be read only by that account. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings, under Account. That permanently deletes your account and the campaigns synced to it; the campaigns on your own devices stay. You can also ask us to delete it by email (below).

Purchases

If you buy The Almanac on Android, the payment is handled by Google Play. On the web, payment is handled by Paddle, which acts as the seller and handles payment and sales tax. We never see your card or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and make it available on your devices, Skyscribe uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an identifier for your app or your account (not your name or email), and basic technical information such as the app version and device type.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Paddle's privacy policy, Supabase's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Children

Skyscribe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
